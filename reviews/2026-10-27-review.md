# Forest Survivor review, 27 October 2026 (at bf524cf)

Read-only: three tracks, 123 agents. Every bug was checked by two skeptics; design and engagement findings were fact-checked against the code and the owner's settled rules. Nothing was run or changed.

## Game design (D)

**D1. Six small screen and input fixes, each approvable on its own (D1a-D1f)**
- **D1a.** Every level-up screen says "+0% damage · +0% health". `public/js/ui/gamescreen.js:372-377` builds that text, but level gains have been 0 since 21 Oct (`public/js/game/player.js:28-36`). The screen should only show the bonus when it isn't zero, and otherwise show "LEVEL n · choose one".
- **D1b.** Quitting a run shows "YOU HAVE FALLEN" with the death backdrop. The title and colours check only `res.victory` (`gamescreen.js:1469-1472, 1510-1511`), though `res.died` already exists (`public/js/game/game.js:1866, 1872`). A quit should get a neutral title such as "RUN ENDED · gold kept". This item was in the 17 Oct review (item 5) and is still open.
- **D1c.** Restarting after a run takes 5-6 presses. Focus lands on the hero card and then the trial card, not on PLAY and BEGIN (`gamescreen.js:1634-1637`, `public/js/ui/hero-selection.js:56, 79`, `public/js/ui/screens.js:285, 311`). Focus should start on PLAY and BEGIN when a choice is already remembered.
- **D1d.** Swarm warning arrows use the same red and skull as the boss pointer (`public/js/game/hud.js:1012` vs `:1023`), although the icons were added so markers could be told apart (`hud.js:204-207`). Warnings should get their own icon and an orange colour.
- **D1e.** The ultimate button is shown as "X", but on PlayStation pads button 2 is Square. Nothing reads `gp.id` (`public/js/engine/input.js:21, 117-128`; `hud.js:663`; `public/js/game/hints.js:23`; `gamescreen.js:634-635`). The game should detect PS pads and show their symbols. Pad B should also close the pause screen (`gamescreen.js:1287`).
- **D1f.** There is no reduced-flash option. Full-screen white flashes reach 0.85 alpha (`game.js:1118, 1166, 1607, 1675, 1690, 2434-2438`), and crit numbers change colour 16 times a second (`public/js/game/numbers.js:250`). Add an option "FLASHES: FULL / SOFT": flashes capped at 0.2, steady gold crits, no white strobe on the evolution ceremony or the boss bar.

Effort: S each. Conflicts with: D1c only. db3987d decided that every run starts from the hero screen. The route stays the same; only where focus starts changes.

**D2. The Butcher's charge goes past its warning line**
The red line is a fixed 200 px long and 26 px wide (`public/js/game/bosses.js:167`, drawn at that exact length in `public/js/game/effects.js:1142-1157`). The dash covers 290 × diff.speed × move × foePace × 0.7 s (`bosses.js:140-148`, `public/js/game/enemies.js:353, 397-400`). That is about 345 px on Elite, 248 px on Veteran and 156 px on Normal. A hero standing just past the red end still gets hit, for 1.4× contact damage (`enemies.js:511`). The contact half-width is 17 px against 13 px drawn. This breaks the rule at `bosses.js:2` that every boss hit can be dodged.
Change: work out the line's length from the real dash distance, and set its width to 2 × (e.r + p.r).
Effort: S.

**D3. Show evolution paths honestly**
A weapon card always says its partner relic is "owned" or "missing" (`gamescreen.js:142, 526, 558`). It never checks whether the relic is still locked or whether a relic slot is free. For a new profile Scholar's Crown (Raise Dead, Scythe) needs level 40, the Hourglass needs 8:00, the Eagle Feather needs 2,000 kills and the Spellbook needs 4:00 (`public/js/shared/progress.js:67-73`). With 3 relic slots (`progress.js:19`) a player can chase an evolution that cannot happen this run. Partner relics also get no extra weight in the card pool (`game.js:485`). The pause screen shows no recipes either (`gamescreen.js:1255-1269`). `evolutionRecipe` already exists, but only the Codex uses it (`public/js/ui/menu-copy.js:58-62`). The 17 Oct review's item 2 is still open.
Change:
- The hint gets three states: owned, can still come, or blocked ("Locked: reach level 40" or "No relic slot").
- A partner relic of a held weapon that hasn't evolved yet gets ×2.5 weight.
- The pause screen shows the partner relic's icon after each weapon, ticked or dimmed, plus "LV n/8".

Effort: S.

**D4. Unlocking more weapons makes the hero's signature weapon rarer at level-up**
Every held weapon has weight 3. Every new weapon has weight 1 and every new relic 0.55 (`game.js:475, 478, 485`). While a weapon slot is free, the signature's chance per card falls from 29% on a new profile to 8% with everything unlocked. Once the slots are full it is about 15-20%. Runs start with no rerolls or banishes (`progress.js:22-23`). Progress makes the class-defining weapon slower to grow, and evolving it is also what opens the Phoenix Heart.
Change: the hero's own unique weapons get weight 6. If the signature hasn't been offered on two screens in a row, the next screen offers it (the same idea as `sureRelic`).
Effort: S.

**D5. Normal has Veteran's bosses but an eighth of the XP to meet them**
NORMAL copies VETERAN and changes only spawn, horde and pace (`public/js/data/difficulty.js:101-110`). Bosses and champions scale only with hp (`game.js:799-801, 851`; `public/js/game/spawner.js:398`), and gems pay the same XP on every tier (`game.js:968`). By 4:30 Normal has spawned about 460 monsters against 3,270 on Veteran, but the Bone King has 6,362 hp on both. Normal is the default tier and the first thing itch players will see. Its events are also hollow: the "Wolves on the hunt!" banner brings 2 wolves (`spawner.js:335`, `events.js:15`).
Change:
- Give Normal its own boss and champion health factor, for example bossHp 0.35 and eliteHp 0.5.
- Pack events always bring at least 6 monsters.
- Optionally answer an open question while you're here: should enemy shots slow with pace? They don't now (`public/js/game/projectiles.js:416-417, 449-450`), so on Normal the Cultist bolt (61 px/s) keeps pace with a walking Sorcerer (59.5 px/s).

Effort: S. Conflicts with: difficulty-tiers.md says "Champions, bosses ... unchanged" on Normal, and leaves "attacks/projectiles unchanged" as an unanswered question. gameplay-calls says the grind is by design. Your call.

**D6. Kill and level unlocks follow horde size, not the player's skill or play time**
The Codex locks count raw kills and the level reached (`progress.js:58-100`). XP is the same on every tier (`enemies.js:180`), but Normal spawns 1/8 of Veteran's horde (`difficulty.js:105-106`) and is the default (`public/js/engine/storage.js:297`). An example 8-minute Veteran death (about 9,000 kills, level 42, one boss) opens 17 of the 24 non-deep locks at once (`public/js/shared/profile.js:596-601`). The results screen then cuts the list off at "+N more" (`gamescreen.js:1355`). A 5-minute Normal run opens 4. Exclusion slots show the same problem: 7 slots after one Normal run, then the 16th needs 327,680 kills (`profile.js:417-425`). `setHome` also lets a new player leave every open shared weapon at home (`profile.js:438-447`, `screens.js:545`).
Change:
- Count kills in Veteran-equivalent units (each kill adds 1/diff.spawn to a new `p.effort` counter) and spread the thresholds so about 2-4 locks open per run.
- Cap exclusion slots at about 5, with spread-out thresholds.
- Always leave at least one shared weapon in play.
- Decide together with this whether time unlocks count on Normal (an open question in difficulty-tiers.md).

Effort: M.

**D7. Upgrade cards show bare changes with no starting value and ignore the hero**
A card shows only its text, for example "Damage {+2}" on the 42-damage Longsword (about +5%), next to Gauntlets' "Damage {+10%}" (`gamescreen.js:136-141`; `public/js/data/weapons.js:46-54`; `public/js/data/passives.js:4`). Relic cards use fixed text (`gamescreen.js:145-149`). On the Knight, Troll Blood says 0.3 a second but gives 0.6, because regenMul is 2 (`public/js/data/heroes.js:27`, `player.js:181`). The Paladin's healMul and the Witch Hunter's bane don't show anywhere either.
Change: add one "before → after" line, for example "Damage 46 → 48 (+4%)", "Cooldown 0.90s → 0.72s" or "Damage +20% → +30%". Show numbers adjusted for the hero, and add a small "Synergy: <perk>" tag in the hero's colour where a card works with the hero's perk.
Effort: M.

**D8. Once the build is full, every level-up is the same Blessing**
3 weapon slots and 3 relic slots (`progress.js:18-19`) make 38 cards, all drawn by about level 39. Chests draw from the same pool, so the build fills even sooner. After that every level and chest is a filler: Blessing of Valor, +5% might and +2% hp (`player.js:166, 169`; `game.js:550, 740-742`). Meanwhile monster health keeps climbing steeply. Nothing in the run branches. The 17 Oct review's item 9 (a signature mastery branch) is still open.
Change, cheapest first:
- (a) Split Blessing into 2-3 kinds (might, cooldown, area). ALWAYS can still auto-pick one of them.
- (b) Maxed and evolved weapons get 3 "overcharge" ranks each.
- (c) Each signature gets two mutually exclusive mastery cards at L8. Start with one hero.

Keep the ALWAYS auto-pick path, and keep the rule that chests never give a new item while held items can still rise.
Effort: M.

**D9. A surprise jolt can be about 74 Cultists, Ogres or Death Knights**
Jolts pick their monster from the current mix (`spawner.js:382-387`). On Elite that means about 58 monsters at 5:00 and 74 at 12:00, locked on for 6 s at 1.2× speed (`spawner.js:335, 362-363`). From 11:00 a jolt can be 74 Ogres with about 12k hp each, unwarned. That breaks "Cultists are few, big and slow". On Veteran and Normal a shooter or charger that survives the swap in `pickType` is cut by `few` a second time (`spawner.js:145-154, 335`).
Change: jolts draw only from the swift and quick kinds in the current mix, with no shooters, Ogres or Death Knights. Skip the second `few` cut for anything chosen by `pickType`.
Effort: S.

**D10. Ranked: rename it, fix its flat health curve, and warn about Elite**
Ranked keeps personal bests only (`screens.js:814, 360-362`). Its seed is `Math.random` (`game.js:181`), so a player reading "Ranked" will expect a leaderboard. Its monsters use the older `hpScale`, not the night curve (`public/js/data/waves.js:72-85`). At 15:00 they have ×6.58 health against ×17.28 in Survival. They reach Survival's dawn health only around minute 29. After dawn the mix only loses kinds (`waves.js:26`). Ranked is always Elite (`difficulty.js:117`), but nothing points a Normal player at Elite first (`profile.js:371-383`).
Change:
- (a) Show the mode as "Endless" and keep the id `ranked`.
- (b) Use `nightHpScale` up to phase 21, then let `hpScale` keep growing from there.
- (c) Show an "Elite: full strength" warning the first time it opens, and have `nextGoal` suggest one Elite night first.

This would ship unmeasured under the no-testing rule.
Effort: S.

**D11. Dungeon Runs give no gear**
`sanitizeRun` forces found = 0 for the Dungeon (`profile.js:493`). Floor guardians drop exactly what a Survival boss drops (`game.js:1127-1130`), and floors 1-2 reuse the 4:30 and 9:00 Survival bosses. Once the ladder is done, Survival is the better mode to build a hero in. The 17 Oct review's item 11 is only partly done.
Change: each guardian drops one guaranteed piece of gear, with rarity set by floor depth (`rollItem`, no luck, as crypt lords do). Raise the Dungeon cap to found ≤ 3.
Effort: S.

**D12. No per-hero difficulty marks or mastery goals**
A win sets one bit per hero, whatever the difficulty (`profile.js:52, 570-577`; feats are capped at 15 at `:168`). Cards draw the same star for a Normal win and an Elite win (`public/js/ui/ladder.js:57-63`). Records adds wins across difficulties together (`screens.js:801-802`). Every hero records a first evolution, but only the Knight's opens anything (`progress.js:75`). After all 9 heroes are recruited, there is no reason to go back to one.
Change: add Veteran, Elite and Nightmare win marks per hero for Survival and the Dungeon, drawn as bronze, silver and gold stars plus a red skull for Nightmare. Add a heroes × difficulties grid in Records and a 6-step mastery row on the hero screen using data that already exists. This also covers the old review's item 9.
Effort: M. Conflicts with: hero-looks (one look per hero). Only if the mastery reward reuses Shadow B or Necro B as skins.

**D13. Explain deaths and teach the first level-up**
`killedBy` is just the last blow, and in a crowd of 2,500 that is often a rat (`player.js:432, 477`). `fromBoss`, `fromMobs` and `fromShots` are recorded, but only the bot driver reads them. The prologue turns off gems (`public/js/game/prologue.js:134`), so the first level-up screen comes with no explanation. The weapons hint fires only after that screen closes (`hints.js:61`). Hints count as "said" as soon as they are queued, and can be pushed out unseen (`hints.js:47-53`; `game.js:54, 1556-1562`). `done()` marks all of them said, even ones never shown (`hints.js:70-77`). The 17 Oct review's items 1 and 5 are still partly open.
Change:
- (a) Results gets a "Wounds: Cultist bolts 38% · Wolves 22% · Bone King 15%" row and one tip.
- (b) A gem hint on the first pickup.
- (c) A one-line footer the first time the level-up screen opens.
- (d) A hint counts as said only once its banner has fully played.

Effort: M. Conflicts with: (d) changes the "all count as said after run one" design in the `hints.js` header. That is a code comment, not your quote, but please confirm.

**D14. Night bosses arrive on top of horde spikes, and the Bone King's second phase outruns slow heroes**
The steady horde never pauses for a boss (`spawner.js:182`). The Bone King at 4:30 gets the 5:00 bat batch. The Butcher at 9:00 lands on the same second as the +30% late horde, the first Death Knights and the steep part of the health curve (`waves.js:20, 80-82, 128-133`). The Lich at 13:00 follows the 12:51 Wraith ring, which is 993 on Veteran and 1,527 on Elite. The arrival shove (`arrival.js:12-13`) pushes the horde back once and then stops. In phase 2 the Bone King moves at 60 × 1.12 × 1.3 = 87 px/s (`bosses.js:114`), faster than every hero's base speed (70-84 px/s).
Change:
- (a) While a night boss stands, the horde runs at 40% and timed events wait.
- (b) Move the imp batch to 10:30, the Wraith ring to 12:00, and the late horde to 9:30.
- (c) Bone King phase 2 moves in bursts: a 0.5 s shudder, then 1.5 s at ×1.3, then a stop.

Effort: M. Conflicts with: your 26 Oct rule that the horde comes at a steady rate whatever stands (`waves.js:35-37`); "last 6 minutes" for the late horde (`difficulty.js:58-63`); "Bone King needs to be faster" (`public/js/data/enemies.js:55-57`; spd 60 stays).

**D15. The night's last four minutes bring nothing new, and the Lich can simply be outlasted**
After 11:00 the mix adds no new monster (`waves.js:22-24`). The Lich arrives at 13:00 with about 178k hp on Elite and stays 85-160 px away (`bosses.js:232-237`). Dawn wins whether he stands or not (`game.js:2124, 1857`). The last imp batch spills past dawn and is dropped (`spawner.js:182`). The peak of the night comes at 11:00, and dawn feels flat.

## Bugs

Each bug below was checked by two adversarial verifiers. The verdict and severity are theirs. Several related findings are merged into one item; the severity shown is the highest part. All the evidence comes from reading the code at `bf524cf`. Nothing was run.

### High

**B1. Dungeon Run: the stairs open on the guardian's loot, and stepping on them throws away the boss chest and elixir.** CONFIRMED · high
- **Now:** `bossDefeated` drops the boss chest, coins, goldpile and elixir at `at`, then opens the stairs on the same tile (`public/js/game/game.js:1126-1134`, `public/js/game/dungeon.js:1089-1105`).
- The stairs fire on the first frame the hero touches them. Crypt doors wait for an `armed` flag; these stairs have none (`public/js/game/dungeon.js:1009-1014`).
- A chest can only be taken after 0.82 game-seconds (`public/js/game/pickups.js:51,176`). The kill's slow motion stretches that to about 2 real seconds.
- `enterFloor` then wipes the pickups without sweeping them first (`public/js/game/game.js:373`). A melee hero who kills the guardian is usually already standing on the stairs, so the gold chest (3-5 upgrades or an evolution) is lost silently on floors 1 and 2.
- **Fix:** sweep the floor before `enterFloor` clears it, the way `sweepCrypt` does: chests go to `chestQueue`, gems become XP, coins become gold, and the elixir is applied. Also open the stairs 2 or more tiles away from `at`, and arm them only after the hero has stepped off or the chest is taken.
- **Effort:** S

### Medium

**B2. Chests and gear picked up during dawn or victory are lost, including the Dungeon's final boss chest.** CONFIRMED · medium
- **Now:** chest and gear queues are only opened while `state === 'play'` (`public/js/ui/gamescreen.js:97-108`). During the 5 s dawn the hero still walks and collects (`public/js/game/game.js:2043,2090`).
- Nothing settles the queues in `finish()` (`public/js/game/game.js:1865-1891`), so the chest's gold and any gear never reach the results.
- On floor 3, `victory('dungeon')` runs in the same call that drops the final boss chest (`public/js/game/game.js:1127,1138`), so that chest can never be opened.
- The 17 Oct casino review asked for a test of "a victory with pending rewards" (`reviews/casino-review.md:175`). This is that case, and it is still open.
- **Fix:** in `finish()`, settle what is left. Roll every queued chest so its gold counts, and auto-sell queued gear at `itemPrice` or list it on the results screen. Also stop `Pickup.update` collecting chests and items outside `play`. Share the sweep code with B1.
- **Effort:** S

**B3. The game does not pause when it loses focus. On itch.io the horde keeps hitting a hero who can't move.** CONFIRMED · medium
- **Now:** the only focus handling is `blur`, which clears held keys (`public/js/engine/input.js:180`). Nothing listens for `visibilitychange`.
- itch.io runs the game in an iframe. A click on the page around it, or an alt-tab to another monitor, keeps the game loop running while the hero stands still. Since quitting ends the run, the run is simply lost.
- **Fix:** on window `blur`, or when the document becomes hidden, open the existing PauseOverlay if the state is `play` and no overlay is up. Use one `pause()` helper shared with the Esc path (`public/js/ui/gamescreen.js:87-90`).
- **Effort:** S

**B4. Changing world (stairs, crypt door, faint) drops state that should carry over.** CONFIRMED · medium for Leech Chains, low for the rest
- **Now:** `enterFloor` and `clearTransient` keep only `persistent` effects (`public/js/game/game.js:376,1392`). Four things break:
  - **Leech Chains and Blood Tether go quiet.** Dropped chains keep their slots, because their foes are pooled, not dead, and keep their old ids (`public/js/game/weapons.js:598`). After a floor change the weapon can fire nothing for seconds up to minutes.
  - **Ultimates lose their effect.** The effect (Whirl, Sanctum, Frenzy and the rest) is deleted, but `p.ult` keeps running, and `enterFloor` also removes Army of the Dead (`public/js/game/game.js:372`).
  - **A crypt lord killed by an arrow or bolt still in flight after the hero starts leaving loses its drops.** `leaveCrypt` sweeps before the 0.6 s fade (`public/js/game/game.js:1306`). The lord's gear and chest are then thrown away, while the banner says "dropped" and the crypt counts as cleared.
  - **A boss's "stir" is lost.** Entering a crypt during it nulls the stir but not `stirFor` (`public/js/game/game.js:1258`, `public/js/game/spawner.js:295`), so the darkened edges never come back.
- **Fix:**
  - Reset `w.chains = []` for every weapon in `enterFloor` and `clearTransient`.
  - Mark the ultimate effects that are centred on the hero as `persistent`. For Overgrowth, Arrow Rain and Army of the Dead on a floor change, end `p.ult` cleanly and fire the receipt.
  - Move `sweepCrypt` into the travel callback, just before `backToForest`.
  - Set `stirFor = -1` in `enterCrypt`.
- **Effort:** S

**B5. The Hex curse's payoff and Ember Heart's burn apply the hero's damage multipliers twice.** CONFIRMED · medium
- **Now:** `curseRec` stores damage that already includes every multiplier (`public/js/game/game.js:927`). The payoff divides out only might, then goes back through `hitEnemy`, which applies fury, Wild Mushroom, brand, bane, Torc, rage and grove again (`public/js/game/enemies.js:317-320`).
- Ember Heart passes the final `d`, crit included, into `addDot`, and each tick multiplies it again (`public/js/game/game.js:933`, `public/js/game/enemies.js:331`). Every other damage-over-time source passes raw weapon stats.
- This pushes Hex Hourglass and Ember above their balance bands.
- **Fix:** add an `o.raw` flag to `hitEnemy` that skips the hero-side multipliers, and use it for the curse payoff. Pass the blow before multipliers to Ember's `addDot`. Then re-check both with `tools/dps.mjs`.
- **Effort:** S
- **Conflicts with:** weapon-balance-bands. The fix brings both weapons back toward their bands, so expect them to measure lower.

**B6. Crowd-control rules: frozen casters and freeze immunity.** Historical findings · medium

**Owner clarification, 15 November 2026:** swarms deliberately retain their forced direction ahead of fear and confusion. They cross the screen with their own movement for six seconds and receive a 20% speed boost over their otherwise configured speed. This is intended gameplay, not a defect; the earlier recommendation to change that priority is withdrawn. Frozen-caster handling and pooled shatter state have since been fixed; champion/lord freeze immunity remains a separate balance decision.
- **Now:**
  - **Frozen casters shoot.** `caster()` has no freeze check, so frozen Cultists and Skeleton Archers wind up and fire. The red wind-up flash is hidden under the ice tint (`public/js/game/enemies.js:671-697,758-763`). `charger()` does have the check (`public/js/game/enemies.js:658`).
  - **Champions and lords freeze solid.** The freeze in `hitEnemy` exempts only bosses and props (`public/js/game/game.js:919`). Every other freeze only slows "the strong" (`public/js/game/game.js:1599,1679`), but Frozen Heart can keep a champion or crypt lord frozen for as long as it is in reach.
  - **`shatterOnDeath` is never reset.** It survives in pooled foes (`public/js/game/enemies.js:148`; `init` never clears it), so Frost Rune and Rimeheart freezes set off Frozen Heart shatters.
- **Fix:**
  - In `caster()`, return early while frozen.
  - Add `!e.lord && !e.elite` to the freeze at `public/js/game/game.js:919`, with a slow as the fallback.
  - Set `shatterOnDeath = false` in `init()`.
- **Effort:** S
- **Owner call:** Thunderstorm's stun can already hold a champion that is not a lord. Decide whether champions, as well as lords, should resist hard crowd control.

**B7. Dungeon Run follow-ups to "monsters come straight through walls".** CONFIRMED · medium
- **Now:** commit `decca8b` changed only monster movement, and four things no longer fit:
  - **Cultists hide in the rock.** They hover about 75 px off inside walls. They never get a line of sight, so they never shoot and never come out (`public/js/game/enemies.js:687-696`).
  - **Auto-aim fires into walls.** It has no line-of-sight check (`public/js/game/game.js:93,1506-1530`), and shots break on the wall face (`public/js/game/projectiles.js:235`, `public/js/game/weapons.js:201-206`). Fireballs, daggers, rays, roots and Thorns are wasted on foes inside rock. The bow is not affected.
  - **Necromancer minions stick to walls** while chasing those foes (`public/js/game/minions.js:178-190`).
  - **Floor 3 lava pools can trap rewards.** They leave open tiles walled in by lava, and shrines or chests can land on them, out of reach (`public/js/game/dungeon.js:366-368,1411-1446`).
- **Fix:**
  - When `throughWalls` is set and a caster has no line of sight, let it keep advancing.
  - Make `nearestEnemy` and `nearestEnemies` prefer foes standing on open floor, and use any foe only if none is in reach. Use the same filter in `Minion.best`.
  - After laying a lava pool, turn any open tile with no walkable neighbour into lava.
- **Effort:** M
- **Conflicts with:** the 27 Oct rule that the horde comes straight through walls. These fixes keep that rule and change only how the hero's side reacts.

**B8. The anti-cheat gold cap of 12 gold a second silently cuts real gold, and the results line no longer adds up.** CONFIRMED · medium
- **Now:** `MAX_GOLD_PER_SEC = 12` has not changed since the first commit (`public/js/shared/profile.js:103,510`). It ignores everything added since: Armory Greed (up to ×1.8), ring greed, Cursed Skulls (up to ×2.5), Fortune, and the gold filler, which grows with phase (`public/js/game/game.js:594`).
- The cap also applies in local mode, the mode the itch build uses (`public/js/engine/storage.js:179`). Long Ranked runs and greedy builds lose gold.
- The results screen prints the uncapped "Collected N" next to a capped "+N GOLD" (`public/js/ui/gamescreen.js:1618`).
- **Fix:**
  - Scale the cap by what the save can check: Armory Greed rank, worn ring greed, the most skulls allowed, and phase growth. Or raise the constant a lot.
  - Return the gold actually credited in `reward`, and print that figure.
- **Effort:** S-M

**B9. Frame hitches at the 2,500 cap.** CONFIRMED · medium. Estimated from the code, not measured.

**C1. The chest's gold and slot payoff plays under a 70% dim**
The HUD is drawn before the overlay (`public/js/ui/gamescreen.js:119`), and `ChestOverlay` starts with a 0.7 black dim (`:761`). **C2. Space (the ultimate key) skips the chest, and a skipped evolution is silent**
