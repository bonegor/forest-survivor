# Reforged hero design and animation review

Each of the 23 playable heroes had a dedicated author and a separate reviewer. Each pair reviewed the design first, then its motion. The reviewer used native Darkwood captures in both facings and a fixed world anchor for stationary attacks. The pair identifiers below are fingerprints of the two distinct sessions.

The finish references are the current Eldrin and Plumed Knight. Every hero keeps its own anatomical baseline, including the spectral Shadow and gaunt Necromancer. [View the original references](media/2026-11-27-hero-style.png).
