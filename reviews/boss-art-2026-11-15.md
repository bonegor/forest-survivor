eight final candidates were accepted. Ea-nāṣir and the two crypt lord types were
excluded following the owner's scope clarification and remain unchanged.

The pass strengthens faces, material separation and character-specific details
with coherent pixel clusters. No gameplay, encounter odds, rewards, attack
events, hit geometry or animation clocks changed. The existing silhouettes and
frame allocations are retained.

## Evidence and preservation

Reviewers inspected all 575 source poses and complete ordered actual-Game
recordings: seven 30-second takes and the Demon Lord's 42-second take, 3,780
captured frames total. Both facings, idle/travel boundaries, attack windups,
release and recovery were covered. Source poseboards supplement frames falling
between the 15 fps video samples; simulation coverage is recorded at 60 Hz.
The Demon Lord also received an independent infernal-ground take.

Lich and Thorn Queen have additional boss-following camera takes to inspect
blink poses outside the normal player viewport. This camera changes only the
review draw and is restored before the next update. All 450 records for each
supplementary take match the normal take in every field except screen-space
draw rectangles. This does not alter the production camera or AI.

Each artist's source/frame audit and the independent reviewers confirm exact
alpha masks and unchanged metadata. The committed geometry baseline was checked
against sprites built from untouched Git revision `0791a0bf`. The new contract
test checks all eight bosses' silhouettes, sizes, anchors, body data, frame maps
and clocks. The six detailed bosses still contain no literal engine OUTLINE
color, preserving the empty-rim allocation fix.

The integration reviewer found no actionable code issue. Its 32 focused tests
passed, including geometry, rim memory, boss attacks and preservation of the
excluded encounters. An isolated offline rebuild reproduced all eight accepted
assets byte for byte. The native Chrome comparison's synchronized tour, pause,
frame-step and facing controls were exercised.

Final clean-snapshot validation: **1,201 tests passed, zero failures/skips**.
The static release build passed with 305 files and 207 module imports checked.
Only this pass's scoped files were overlaid onto `0791a0bf`; unrelated working
tree changes were excluded. The final eight-boss Game gallery was inspected
with the accepted Pit Lord, and every boss retains its distinct identity.

Local evidence is under `output/boss-polish/<boss>/{artist,review}`. The final
real-Game overview is `output/boss-polish/final/eight-bosses.png`. Build and
comparison instructions are in `tools/boss-polish/README.md`.

## Known work retained

The Bone King's original source still clips the sword at some travel/volley
extremes, switches abruptly at travel 14→7, raises an empty-looking hand during
summon, and translates the body in recovery frame 28. Reviewers confirmed these
are inherited animation limitations; this material pass does not repair them.
Addressing them requires an animation and opaque-geometry review.

The larger sprite-memory issue remains open. The six detailed bosses still have
532 frames at 224×192, about 87.3 MiB in decoded RGBA buffers and about 261.8 MiB
including initial normal/mirrored canvases before other variants/GPU storage.
This pass does not enlarge those allocations. Lazy canvas creation/cache lifetime
remains separate work; the empty transparent rim allocation was already fixed
in `0791a0bf`. No risk note is marked resolved merely by repainting the sprites.
