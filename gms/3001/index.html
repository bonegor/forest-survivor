      The screen is the game itself (public/), with the game's own forest, light and HUD. Scouted sprites are redrawn in the game's pixels: heroes as tall as the knight (30 px), every sprite in a monster's part at that part's height. Everything turns as the game turns it (public/js/game/facing.js). "Line up everything" stands every hero class, monster, boss and prop in rows by the hero, labelled with their heights, for comparing sizes; trees stand all round. Red-bordered sprites can't be used in a commercial game. Picks are kept in gms/3001/picks.json. The colour bar on the game recolours what the hero, or the chosen monster part, wears, live; "Save new version" keeps it as a new sprite in that part's list (sprites/lab/versions.json, sheets in sprites/lab/sheets/), with the licence of the sprite it was made from. Double-click a slider to put it back to 0.
