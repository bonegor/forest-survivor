Sprite Lab

The gameCompareMusic scout

Every sprite for one part side by side, at the game's scale with their feet on one line. Try one in the game, or make it the pick.

Sprites are drawn as the game draws them: a proposal in its own pixels, a scouted sprite redrawn in the game's (heroes as tall as the knight, 30 px; a monster at its part's height), each standing on the same ground line. The dashed line is the knight's height for heroes, and the part's for a monster. Red-bordered sprites can't be used in a commercial game. "Try in the game" opens the game wearing it; "Make it the pick" keeps it in gms/3001/picks.json. To add a proposal, put its sheet in sprites/lab/sheets/ and its entry in sprites/lab/catalog.json (see that file's "about").
