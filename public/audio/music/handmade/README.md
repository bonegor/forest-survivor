# Approved handmade music

These are the three MP3 versions approved for inclusion in the Forest Survivor
repository on 28 October 2026. The files are exact copies of the approved local
listening versions, without re-encoding. `manifest.json` records their original
version names, durations, measured levels, source locations and SHA-256 hashes.

| File | Approved version | Tempo | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `spark-rush.mp3` | Spark Rush v1.mp3 | 156 BPM | 2:04 |
| `clockwork-charge.mp3` | Clockwork Charge v2.mp3 | 160 BPM | 2:08 |
| `rally-run-heavy-guitars.mp3` | Rally Run - heavy guitars v1.mp3 | 168 BPM | 2:13 |

The editable compositions, rendering recipes and MIDI scores are in
[`tools/music/handmade-fight`](../../../../tools/music/handmade-fight/README.md).
Adding this library does not change `../tracks.json`, the game's active playlist.

## Credits

Original music composed and programmed for Forest Survivor with the local
Music Studio workflow. All melodies and arrangements are original; no imported
recordings, melodies, vocals, flute or whistle parts are used.

- **Spark Rush:** programmed electronic bass, chords, plucked synth, kick and
  clap, with the installed Cakewalk SI-Drum Kit.
- **Clockwork Charge:** the installed Cakewalk SI-Drum Kit, SI-Bass Guitar,
  SI-String Section and SI-Electric Piano.
- **Rally Run, heavy guitars:** Kontakt/Shreddage 3 Stratus FREE, Guitar Rig's
  Fire Seeker metal amp with matched cabinet, SI-Bass Guitar and SI-Drum Kit.
  Both guitar parts use the metal amp at gain 7, mode II.

Instrument and plugin binaries are not included. Levels and balance were
measured; the assistant did not listen to the audio. The versions above were
selected by the user.
