## Criteria used for this review

Apply the guide's ordering: contour and proportion first; three clear value groups and consistent light direction next; selective detail and clean edges afterward. Judge at actual gameplay size and against the real background, as well as enlarged. Compare the roster for matching pixel density, outlines and lighting. Mirror the sprite and look for equipment/grip problems. Prefer connected clusters over isolated noise; keep the strongest details at the face, weapon and defining contour.

Apply the checklist as visual questions: does the class read from its shape; can the figure separate from the level; do its big shapes survive reduction; does it remain coherent in motion; and does it belong beside the approved Knight? The final critique should distinguish readability defects from taste. Each class gets one highest-impact recommendation, with a concrete shape or pixel change where useful.
